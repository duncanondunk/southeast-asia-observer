# 阿努廷戎装访边境争议区 柬埔寨提最强烈抗议

2026年8月23日，泰国首相阿努廷身着军装，前往泰柬边境争议地区柏威夏省安塞，慰问前线官兵。这场原本被泰方称作"鼓舞士气"的慰问，却在金边引爆迄今为止最强烈的外交抗议——柬埔寨谴责其"侵犯主权"，并称此举不会令泰方的"非法占领"合法化。一场身着戎装的走访，把一段跨越百年的边界之争重新推到火药味最浓的位置。

## 一场"慰问"引发的外交风暴

柬埔寨外交与国际合作部8月25日发文告，就阿努廷"未经授权访问"安塞地区正式提出最强烈抗议。文告措辞罕见地严厉，指这次访问是"企图对柬埔寨主权领土提出非法声索的蓄意行为"，并强调该地区自去年以来持续遭泰国武装部队"非法占领"。

柬方重申，任何非法行为都不会产生合法的领土所有权；因此，泰国首相的到访"绝对不会使非法占领柬埔寨主权领土的行为合法化"。柬方要求泰方立即停止占领，拆除军事设施，让约2万名流离失所的平民返乡。

## 安塞：历史地图之争的焦点

柏威夏省安塞地区，是泰柬边境争议的核心。柬埔寨坚称，依据划分边界的历史地图，安塞完全位于国际公认的柬埔寨领土之内。泰国则长期质疑柬埔寨对这份历史地图的依赖，并对分水岭与边界线有不同解读。

这条边界线并非新争。1962年，海牙国际法院将柏威夏寺判归柬埔寨，但周边地界的划定始终悬而未决，成为两国最敏感的领土议题。安塞只是其中最易擦枪走火的一处。

## 停火之后，摩擦未止

双方的冲突在去年迅速激化。5月、7月与12月，边境至少爆发三次流血冲突，东盟多国乃至中美代表均参与调停。去年12月底，双方签署停火协议，允许边境平民返回家园，但此后摩擦仍时有发生。

阿努廷此行，恰是在停火协议这一脆弱框架之上，又添了一道裂痕。柬方将其解读为对既定边界现状的公开挑战，而非一次单纯的军队慰问。

## 双方各执一词的叙事

泰方给出的说法截然不同。首相府公共关系部称，此行目的是慰问在边境执行防御任务的一线官兵。阿努廷由国防部长与陆军总司令陪同，听取前线局势简报，发放生活物资、鼓舞士气，并探望了一名在排雷勘察中触雷重伤截肢的泰国军人。

泰国陆军亦发文说，视察体现了政府与军队对边境执勤人员的关怀，并全力加强准备以捍卫国家主权；同时致力确保边境地区稳定发展与民众安全。两套叙事，一个在讲"慰问"，一个在读"声索"。

## 划界机制仍在运转

尽管如此，两国政府仍在通过多项双边机制处理悬而未决的划界问题，包括总边界委员会、联合边界委员会与地区边界委员会。柬方也重申，愿同泰方及其他邻国一道，坚持通过和平方式、依据国际法及现有双边协议解决争端。

换言之，外交与军事两条线同时绷紧：一边是前线官兵的物资与掌声，一边是外交部的照会与抗议。安塞的归属一时难有定论，但只要历史地图的解读依旧分歧，这片边境就仍是东南亚最易起火的缝隙之一。

## 延伸阅读

- **安塞地区（An Ses / 安塞）**：柏威夏省边境争议核心区，柬埔寨据历史地图主张主权，泰国则持不同分水岭解读。
- **柏威夏寺争端（Preah Vihear）**：1962年海牙国际法院将柏威夏寺判归柬埔寨，周边边境划定长期未决，是泰柬最敏感的领土议题。
- **总边界委员会（General Boundary Commission）**：泰柬处理划界与边境安全的双边机制之一，与联合、地区边界委员会并行运作。

# Thai PM Anutin's Military-Attire Border Visit Draws Cambodia's Strongest Protest

On 23 August 2026, Thai Prime Minister Anutin visited the contested border zone of An Ses in Preah Vihear province in combat dress to cheer up front-line troops. What Bangkok cast as a morale-boosting visit triggered Phnom Penh's strongest diplomatic protest to date — Cambodia condemned it as an infringement of sovereignty and said it would not legitimise Thailand's "illegal occupation." A walkabout in uniform has pushed a century-old boundary dispute back to its most combustible point in years.

## A 'morale visit' that became a diplomatic storm

Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation issued a statement on 25 August formally lodging its strongest protest over Anutin's "unauthorised visit" to An Ses. The wording was unusually harsh, describing the trip as "a deliberate act attempting to make an illegal claim over Cambodian sovereign territory" and stressing that the area has been "illegally occupied" by Thai armed forces since last year.

Phnom Penh reiterated that no illegal act produces lawful territorial title; therefore, the prime minister's visit "absolutely cannot legitimise the illegal occupation of Cambodian sovereign territory." It demanded that Thailand immediately end the occupation, dismantle military installations, and let roughly 20,000 displaced civilians return home.

## An Ses: the focal point of a map dispute

The An Ses area in Preah Vihear is the heart of the Thailand–Cambodia border contest. Cambodia insists that, under the historical maps used to delimit the boundary, An Ses lies wholly within internationally recognised Cambodian territory. Thailand has long questioned Phnom Penh's reliance on those maps and offers a different reading of the watershed and the line.

This is not a new quarrel. In 1962 the Hague International Court of Justice awarded the Preah Vihear temple to Cambodia, but the surrounding boundary was never settled — the most sensitive territorial issue between the two neighbours. An Ses is simply the flashpoint most prone to sparks.

## After the ceasefire, friction persisted

The conflict escalated sharply last year. At least three bloody clashes erupted in May, July and December, drawing in ASEAN states and even Chinese and US mediators. A ceasefire signed in late December allowed border civilians to return, yet friction has continued.

Anutin's trip added a fresh crack to that already fragile framework. Phnom Penh read it as an open challenge to the status quo, not a routine troop visit.

## Two competing narratives

Thailand tells a very different story. The Prime Minister's Office said the purpose was to comfort front-line defenders. Anutin, joined by the defence minister and army commander, received briefings on the situation, handed out supplies, and visited a Thai soldier who lost a limb to a landmine during a clearance survey.

The Royal Thai Army said the inspection reflected the government's care for border personnel and its resolve to defend sovereignty while ensuring stability and safety. Two narratives, one speaking of "morale," the other reading "claim."

## The delimitation machinery still turns

For all that, both governments continue to handle the unresolved boundary through several bilateral mechanisms — the General, Joint and Regional Boundary Commissions. Cambodia also reaffirmed its readiness to settle the dispute peacefully, with Thailand and other neighbours, under international law and existing agreements.

In short, the diplomatic and military tracks are taut at once: on one side, supplies and applause for the front line; on the other, notes and protests from the foreign ministry. The ownership of An Ses will not be settled soon, but as long as the maps are read differently, this border remains one of Southeast Asia's most flammable seams.
