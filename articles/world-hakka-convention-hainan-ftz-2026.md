# 从「乡情」到「码头」：世界客属恳亲大会为何选中封关后的海南

2026年11月20日，世界客属第34届恳亲大会在海南海口开幕。来自全球各地的客属社团负责人与乡亲代表约2500人出席。这是一场创办于1971年、已经走到第55个年头的老牌华人联谊盛会，但本届大会的主题——「五洲客家音，四海桑梓情」，配上「客聚琼台福地，共享自贸机遇」的口号——透露出与以往不太一样的气味：乡情还是那个乡情，但聚会的理由，已经从「记住我们从哪里来」，悄悄变成了「看看我们能一起做什么」。据中新社报道，海南省委书记冯飞在致辞中直接邀约海内外客属乡亲到海南投资兴业。祠堂与自贸港的招商手册，就这样被摆进了同一个会场。

## 一场55岁的全球乡亲聚会

世客会创办于1971年，是全球极具影响力的华人联谊盛会。55年间，它在世界不同城市间轮流举办，把分散在各地的客家人重新聚到一起。这种聚会的功能本来相当朴素：认亲、叙旧、寻根，让下一代知道自己的祖辈来自哪一座村庄、讲哪一种口音。

但把第34届的议程摊开看，会发现它的重心已经明显位移。开幕式之后是「客行天下·情系中华」艺术展，紧接着是客家青年自贸港创新峰会；当天，代表们分文旅、经贸两条线路在海口参观考察，实地感受核心引领区的「政策优势与产业活力」。闭幕式上还要通报第35届筹备情况、公布第36届承办城市——第35届已定在广西玉林。

这不是批评，而是一个值得记录的变化。联谊还在，但联谊已经不再单独成立，它需要一个「项目」来承载。当侨团大会开始用「创新峰会」和「经贸考察」作骨架，乡情就从目的变成了方法。

## 数字里的「家」：约1亿客家人与近60万海南后裔

看这类大会，不能只看会场里的横幅，还要看数字。

全球客家人约1亿，遍及80余个国家和地区。这是一个相当惊人的分布尺度：它意味着任何以「客属」为名的号召，天然就带有跨国的网络效应。海南现有客家人及后裔近60万人，海口则是客家人入琼的重要登陆门户与早期扩散起点。把大会放在海口，不是随口挑的——这里有可追溯的移民史，也有现成的亲缘网络。

另一组数字来自海南自贸港本身。据冯飞在开幕式上介绍，海南自贸港自2025年12月18日封关以来整体运行平稳有序，「零关税」商品税目由约1900个扩大至约6600个，占全部商品税目的比例从21%提升至74%；88国人员可免签入境海南，今年前10个月入境游客同比增长43.2%。

把这两组数字并排看，逻辑就清楚了：一边是上亿人规模的海外客属网络，一边是一个刚刚把免税税目扩大两倍多、对88个国家开门的自贸港。大会组织者要做的，就是把这两条曲线接在一起。用海口市委书记范少军的话说，所谓「乡情纽带转化为合作纽带，客商网络转化为发展网络」，就是让海口既做全球客属乡亲「永远可以停靠的家」，也做大家「再出发的新码头」。

## 「把资源带进来，把红利带回去」

开幕式上有四位发言者：海南省客家商会会长黄胜、印尼客属联谊总会总主席叶联礼、海南省客属联合会会长颜宪智、广东省客属海外联谊会会长曾智明。其中印尼客属联谊总会总主席叶联礼那句话，最值得拿出来读一遍——本届大会将成为海南自贸港连接全球客商网络的重要契机，「把印尼的资源带进来，把海南的红利带回去」。

这句话直白得近乎朴素，但它其实重新定义了侨务经济的方向。传统的侨乡叙事是单向的：海外乡亲寄钱回来、捐学校、修路，家乡负责感恩与纪念。而「带进来—带回去」是一个双向套利的结构：侨网络不再只是汇款通道，而是资源与政策红利的搬运工。印尼的资源——无论是大宗商品、市场渠道还是华商资本——经由客属网络进入海南；海南的零关税、免签与产业优惠，则经由同一张网络回流到东南亚。

据报道，发言的印尼客属组织并非孤例，全球客属社团负责人几乎都在这张网里。问题在于：这种双向流动能不能持续？它依赖的不是一次大会的热度，而是制度性差价能否长期存在。只要海南与周边地区之间还有可套利的空间，这张网络就会自己运转；一旦政策趋同，乡情也就只剩乡情了。

## 从停靠的「家」到再出发的「码头」

「永远可以停靠的家」和「再出发的新码头」，这是两个意味深长的比喻，官方把它用在了同一句话里。

「家」意味着情感、归属和不计成本的接纳；「码头」意味着中转、补给和继续上路。把两者叠在同一个城市身上，其实是在给海南自贸港重新定位：它不只是海外客家人「落叶归根」的终点，更是他们「再出发」的起点。这个定位很聪明，因为它避开了侨务工作里一个长期的尴尬——如果家乡只提供情感回报，那么随着第二代、第三代华裔与祖籍地的疏远，情感的续航力会越来越弱；但如果家乡同时提供商业机会，关系就有了自我维持的动力。

这也是为什么本届大会特意安排了客家青年自贸港创新峰会。青年是离散网络里最容易「断线」的一环，让他们参与创新与创业，比让他们参观祠堂更能维系联系。乡情要年轻化，就得先让它「有用」。

不过也要看到另一面。当「家」被改写成「码头」，归属感就被赋予了明确的使用说明。接待方关心的是签约额与项目落地，侨团关心的是政策红利与进入渠道，双方各取所需——这是健康的交换，但如果只剩下交换，那么「桑梓情」三个字很快就会变成一句客套。

## 乡情能否承载自贸港的野心

把一届恳亲大会办成招商大会，世客会不是第一次，海南也不是第一个。真正的新变量在于时机：这是海南自贸港全岛封关运作后的首个完整年度。封关改变了规则，规则改变了套利空间，而侨网络恰好是最擅长发现和利用空间差的那类网络。

所以，本届大会最值得追问的不是「开了几天、来了多少人」，而是「两年后还有多少项目活着」。乡情降低了交易的信任成本，这是它的真实价值；但乡情不能替代产业逻辑，更不能替代制度的稳定性。一个自贸港要吸引海外客商，最终靠的不是乡亲见面时的热情，而是税目表能不能兑现、审批能不能预期、纠纷能不能公正解决。

据中新社报道，第35届世客会已确定在广西玉林举办，第36届承办城市将在闭幕式上公布。一届一届办下去，这张网络会继续为地方发展输送连接。但发生在海口的这届提供了一个样本：当乡情被明确写进招商手册，我们要观察的不只是它促成了多少投资，还有它有没有因为过度使用而变薄。把亲情当资源来用，前提是别把它用成了消耗品。

## 延伸阅读

**客属恳亲大会（World Hakka Convention）**：1971年创办的全球客家人联谊盛会，轮流在各地举办，兼具宗亲联络、文化交流与经贸对接功能。

**海南自贸港封关（Hainan Free Trade Port customs closure）**：2025年12月18日起实施的全岛独立海关监管安排，以零关税、低税率、简税制为核心政策框架。

**侨力经济（diaspora economy）**：以海外乡亲的资本、网络与专业技能为纽带，把情感认同转化为投资、贸易与人才流动的发展模式。

# From Ancestral Ties to a "New Pier": Why the World Hakka Convention Chose Post-Closed-Customs Hainan

On 20 November 2026, the 34th World Hakka Convention opened in Haikou, capital of south China's island province of Hainan, with some 2,500 delegates — leaders of Hakka associations and community representatives — in attendance. It is a veteran gathering: the convention was founded in 1971 and has now reached its 55th year. Yet the theme of this edition, "Hakka voices from five continents, the affection of a homeland across four seas", paired with the slogan "Hakka gather in Qiong's auspicious land, sharing the opportunities of the free trade port", carries a noticeably different scent. The sentiment is familiar; the reason for gathering has quietly shifted from remembering where we came from to asking what we can build together. According to China News Service, Hainan Party secretary Feng Fei used his opening address to invite Hakka compatriots at home and abroad to invest in the province. Ancestral halls and free-trade-port investment brochures found themselves in the same room.

## A 55-Year-Old Global Reunion

Founded in 1971, the World Hakka Convention is among the most influential Chinese diaspora gatherings in the world. Over 55 years it has rotated among cities across continents, reassembling a community scattered by migration. Its original function was plain enough: to recognise kin, swap stories, trace roots, and let the next generation learn which village and which accent their ancestors called their own.

Spread out the programme of the 34th edition, however, and the centre of gravity has visibly moved. After the opening came an art exhibition titled "Hakka Across the World, Heart Bound to China", followed immediately by a Hakka Youth Free Trade Port Innovation Summit. That same day delegates toured Haikou along two routes — culture and tourism, and trade and investment — to see first-hand the "policy advantages and industrial vitality" of the island's core leading zone. The closing ceremony would report on preparations for the 35th convention and announce the host city of the 36th; the 35th is already fixed for Yulin, in the neighbouring region of Guangxi.

This is not a criticism, but a change worth recording. The reunion remains, yet it no longer stands on its own — it needs a "project" to carry it. When a diaspora convention takes an innovation summit and an investment tour as its backbone, sentiment stops being the endpoint and becomes the method.

## The "Home" in Numbers: 100 Million Hakka and 600,000 in Hainan

To read a gathering like this, look beyond the banners and at the numbers.

There are roughly 100 million Hakka worldwide, spread across more than 80 countries and territories. That is a striking scale: any call issued in the name of "Hakka" carries an inherently transnational network effect. Hainan itself is home to nearly 600,000 Hakka and their descendants, and Haikou was an important landing point and early staging post for Hakka migration onto the island. Hosting the convention there was no arbitrary choice — the city has a traceable migration history and a ready-made kinship network.

The second set of numbers concerns the free trade port itself. According to Feng Fei's opening remarks, since the island-wide customs closure took effect on 18 December 2025, Hainan has operated smoothly: its zero-tariff item list has expanded from about 1,900 to about 6,600, raising the share of all tariff lines covered from 21% to 74%. Nationals of 88 countries can now enter visa-free, and inbound visitors grew 43.2% year on year in the first ten months of this year.

Set the two sets side by side and the logic becomes clear: on one side, an overseas Hakka network on the order of 100 million people; on the other, a free trade port that has more than tripled its tariff-free catalogue and opened its doors to 88 countries. The organisers' task is to connect the two curves. What Fan Shaojun, Haikou's Party secretary, called turning "an affectionate bond into a cooperative bond, and a network of Hakka merchants into a network of development" means making Haikou both "a home always open for return" and "a new pier for setting out again".

## "Bring Resources In, Take the Dividends Back"

Four speakers took the stage at the opening: Huang Sheng of the Hainan Hakka Chamber of Commerce, Ye Lianli, chairman-general of the Indonesia Hakka Association, Yan Xianzhi of the Hainan Hakka Federation, and Zeng Zhiming of the Guangdong Overseas Chinese Hakka Friendship Association. The line worth reading twice came from Ye: this convention, he said, would be a crucial chance for Hainan's free trade port to connect with global Hakka business networks — "bringing Indonesia's resources in and Hainan's dividends back out".

Blunt to the point of plainness, the sentence quietly redefines the direction of diaspora economics. The traditional qiaoxiang narrative runs one way: overseas compatriots send money home, fund schools, build roads, and the homeland responds with gratitude and commemoration. "Bring in, take back" is a two-way arbitrage structure. The diaspora network is no longer merely a remittance channel; it becomes a courier of resources and policy dividends. Indonesian resources — commodities, market access, Hakka capital — travel through the network into Hainan, while Hainan's zero tariffs, visa-free entry and industrial incentives travel back out along the same routes to Southeast Asia.

As reporting showed, the Indonesian body was hardly alone; leaders of Hakka associations from around the world were all inside this web. The question is whether such two-way flows can last. What sustains them is not the warmth of a single conference but whether a durable institutional price gap exists. So long as a spread remains between Hainan and its surroundings, the network will keep running by itself; once policies converge, affection will be all that is left.

## From a "Home" to Dock at to a Pier to Depart From

"A home always open for return" and "a new pier for setting out again" are two loaded metaphors, and the official language placed them in a single sentence.

A "home" implies emotion, belonging, and welcome without calculation. A "pier" implies transit, resupply, and departure. Stacking both on one city is a way of repositioning the free trade port: not merely the endpoint of a Hakka's "return to the roots", but the starting point of a new journey. The framing is clever because it sidesteps a long-standing awkwardness in diaspora work. If the homeland offers only emotional rewards, the bond weakens as second- and third-generation descendants drift from the ancestral place; if it also offers commercial opportunity, the relationship acquires a self-sustaining engine.

That is why this edition made a point of staging a Hakka Youth Free Trade Port Innovation Summit. Young people are the link most likely to break in a diaspora network; letting them into innovation and business keeps the connection alive far better than a tour of ancestral halls. For ancestral sentiment to stay young, it must first be made useful.

There is another side, though. Once "home" is recast as "pier", belonging is issued with an instruction manual. Hosts look at signed deals and landed projects; associations look at policy dividends and access channels. Both sides get what they came for — a healthy exchange in itself — but if exchange is all that remains, "the affection of a homeland" quickly becomes a polite phrase.

## Can Ancestral Sentiment Carry a Free Trade Port's Ambition?

Turning a kinship convention into an investment conference is not new, either for the World Hakka Convention or for Hainan. The genuinely new variable is timing: this is the first full year after the island-wide customs closure. Closure changed the rules; the rules changed the arbitrage space; and diaspora networks happen to be among the best at spotting and exploiting such gaps.

So the most important question about this edition is not how many days it ran or how many people attended, but how many of its projects will still be alive two years from now. Kinship lowers the trust cost of a transaction — that is its real value. But kinship cannot substitute for industrial logic, still less for institutional stability. A free trade port that wants overseas capital ultimately relies not on the warmth of a reunion, but on whether the tariff schedule is honoured, whether approvals can be anticipated, and whether disputes are settled fairly.

According to China News Service, the 35th convention is set for Yulin in Guangxi, and the host of the 36th will be named at the closing ceremony. Edition by edition, this network will keep feeding connections into local development. What happened in Haikou offers a sample case: when ancestral sentiment is written explicitly into an investment playbook, what deserves watching is not only how much investment it generated, but whether the sentiment was thinned by overuse. Drawing on family feeling as a resource works only if it is not consumed as one.
