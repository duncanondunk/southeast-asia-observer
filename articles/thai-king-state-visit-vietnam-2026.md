# 泰王国国王历史性访越：半世纪邻里情谊的"王室时刻"

## 一场迟到半个世纪的到访

2026年9月14日至16日，泰国国王玛哈·哇集拉隆功（Maha Vajiralongkorn Phra Vajiraklaochaoyuhua）与王后素提达应越南共产党中央委员会总书记、国家主席苏林及夫人的邀请，对越南进行国事访问。9月15日，苏林在河内国会大厦广场为泰国王室伉俪举行欢迎仪式，并检阅越南人民军仪仗队。越南官方将此次访问定义为"两国关系史上具有历史意义的里程碑"——自1976年建交以来，这是泰国君主首次以国事访问的形式踏足越南，也是哇集拉隆功国王与王后即位以来第一次以国家元首身份到访。

## 从储君到国王的越南情缘

国王与王后对越南并不陌生。早在以王储身份活动的年代，哇集拉隆功就曾于1992年与1997年两度访问越南。当年的翩翩储君，如今以一国之君的身份重新踏上这片土地，使得这次访问兼具"首次"与"故地重游"的双重意味。越南《人民报》的社论特意提及，泰国深深铭刻着胡志明主席当年远赴海外寻求救国之路时留下的足迹，这一历史记忆为两国人民的亲近感增添了厚度。王室与越南领导层之间的私人联系，由此被赋予了延续与深化的象征意义。

## 五十年的邻里与伙伴

此次访问落在一个特殊的时间节点：2026年，越南与泰国迎来建交50周年（1976年8月6日）。更关键的是，就在一年前的2025年5月，两国关系刚刚提升至"全面战略伙伴"（Comprehensive Strategic Partnership）。新的框架为双方深化合作提供了制度基础与新鲜动力。在东南亚，泰国与越南同为中南半岛的大国、东盟的核心成员，也是彼此重要的邻邦。建交半世纪之后，双边关系从早期的睦邻友好，逐步走到今天"全面战略伙伴"的高度，此次王室到访，正是这一进程的高光注脚。

## 东盟内最大的贸易纽带

支撑这段关系的不只是礼仪，更有实打实的利益交织。泰国目前是越南在东盟内最大的贸易伙伴。2025年，两国双边贸易额达221亿美元，同比增长9%；其中越南出口88亿美元（增长12.7%），进口133亿美元（增长6.7%）。进入2026年，增速进一步加快——前七个月贸易额已突破150.3亿美元，同比大增22%。投资层面同样紧密：泰国在越南生效投资项目达813个，注册总额超过154亿美元，在153个对越投资国家和地区中排名第8，在东盟投资者中位列第二。经贸，是这场"王室外交"最厚实的底盘。

## 王室外交的象征重量

为什么一场王室访问会被双方如此高规格对待？在东南亚的政治文化中，君主的到访从来不只是礼宾安排，它传递的是国家间最高规格的善意与互信。苏林以党和国家最高领导人的身份亲自接待，泰方则派出由总理阿努廷·查恩维拉库尔（Anutin Charnvirakul）随行的高规格代表团。这种"元首对元首"的安排，把一次常规外交活动升格为政治象征。对河内而言，接待泰王既是对泰国王室在两国友谊中作用的重视，也是在地区格局变动之际，向邻邦释放稳定与亲近的信号。

## 从语言中心到民间暖流

王室行程的细节，透露出"软实力"的取向。据泰国媒体披露，国王与王后计划访问河内大学内的"泰语与泰文化中心"——该中心自2009年起面向社会与学子教授泰语，是民间文化交流的活样本。泰国《每日新闻》《泰叻报》《国民报》等主流媒体连日大篇幅报道，焦点集中在河内行程与两国建交50周年的意涵；泰叻报更将此次访问解读为"亲密友邻之间深化关系"的契机。从语言课堂到媒体叙事，王室外交把高层互信转化成可感知的民间暖流。

## 高层互访的密集年

2026年是越泰高层互动极为频繁的一年。5月，苏林对泰国进行正式访问，双方签署《2026—2031年落实越泰全面战略伙伴关系行动计划》；6月，泰国总理阿努廷访越并出席第三届东盟未来论坛；8月，泰国国会主席兼下议院议长率团访越。短短数月内，从总书记到总理、议长，双方沟通的密度与层级都属罕见。此次泰王国国王的到访，把这一年的高频互动推至顶点——政治互信的巩固，正沿着"领导人—王室—民间"的多条线索同步推进。

## 邻里之间的一课

把视野拉到整个东南亚，泰王访越提供了一则"如何处理邻里关系"的样本。越南与泰国既有历史亲近，也有湄公河水资源、海洋权益等潜在分歧，但双方选择以建交50周年与王室外交为契机，把合作面做大、把分歧管控在桌面上。在区域大国博弈加剧、部分邻国关系趋紧的背景下，两个中南半岛大国以庆典而非对抗来标记彼此的周年，本身就有示范价值。半世纪的邻里情谊，借由一场王室的"高光时刻"，被重新擦亮。

## 延伸阅读

**全面战略伙伴关系（Comprehensive Strategic Partnership）**：国家间最高层级的外交关系定位之一，涵盖政治、安全、经济、人文等全方位合作。越南与泰国于2025年5月将双边关系提升至这一级别。

**东盟（ASEAN）**：东南亚国家联盟，现有十个成员国，是东南亚区域合作与一体化的核心机制；泰国与越南均为创始或早期重要成员。

# Thailand's King Makes History in Vietnam: A Royal Moment for a Half-Century of Neighbourhood

## A visit half a century in the making

From 14 to 16 September 2026, King Maha Vajiralongkorn Phra Vajiraklaochaoyuhua of Thailand and Queen Suthida paid a state visit to Vietnam at the invitation of General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam Central Committee and President To Lam and his spouse. On 15 September, To Lam hosted an official welcome ceremony at the National Assembly House in Hanoi, where the King and Queen reviewed the guard of honour of the Vietnam People's Army. Hanoi framed the visit as "a historic milestone in the relations between the two countries" — since diplomatic ties were established in 1976, this was the first state visit to Vietnam by a Thai monarch, and the first by King Vajiralongkorn and Queen Suthida in their current roles.

## From Crown Prince to King, a Vietnamese bond

The King and Queen were no strangers to Vietnam. As Crown Prince, Vajiralongkorn had visited the country twice, in 1992 and 1997. The dashing prince of those years now returned as head of state, giving the trip the dual character of both a "first" and a "homecoming." The editorial of Vietnam's Nhan Dan newspaper pointedly recalled that Thailand bears the footprints of President Ho Chi Minh from his years abroad seeking a path to national salvation — a historical memory that adds depth to the peoples' affinity. The personal ties between the Royal Family and Vietnam's leadership thus carry a symbolic weight of continuity and deepening.

## Fifty years of neighbours and partners

The visit landed on a special anniversary: in 2026 Vietnam and Thailand marked 50 years of diplomatic relations (6 August 1976). More importantly, just a year earlier, in May 2025, the two countries had elevated their ties to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership, providing an institutional foundation and fresh momentum for deeper cooperation. In Southeast Asia, Thailand and Vietnam are both peninsular powers and core ASEAN members, and important neighbours to each other. Half a century after establishing ties, the relationship has climbed from early good-neighbourliness to today's "comprehensive strategic partnership" — and the royal visit is its high-water mark.

## The largest trade link within ASEAN

What underpins the relationship is not only etiquette but real interlocking interests. Thailand is currently Vietnam's largest trading partner within ASEAN. In 2025 two-way trade reached US$22.1 billion, up 9 per cent; Vietnam's exports stood at US$8.8 billion (up 12.7 per cent) and imports at US$13.3 billion (up 6.7 per cent). In 2026 the pace accelerated further — the first seven months alone exceeded US$15.03 billion, a 22 per cent year-on-year jump. Investment is equally tight: Thailand holds 813 valid projects in Vietnam with registered capital above US$15.4 billion, ranking eighth among 153 countries and territories investing there and second among ASEAN investors. Commerce is the most substantial bedrock of this "royal diplomacy."

## The symbolic weight of royal diplomacy

Why was a royal visit treated with such high ceremony by both sides? In Southeast Asia's political culture, a monarch's presence is never mere protocol — it signals the highest grade of goodwill and mutual trust between states. To Lam received the couple in his capacity as the country's top leader, while Thailand sent a high-level delegation accompanied by Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul. This "head-of-state to head-of-state" arrangement elevates a routine diplomatic event into political symbolism. For Hanoi, hosting the Thai King both honours the Royal Family's role in bilateral friendship and sends a signal of stability and closeness to a neighbour at a moment of shifting regional dynamics.

## From a language centre to people-to-people warmth

The details of the royal itinerary reveal a soft-power bent. Thai media reported that the King and Queen planned to visit the Thai Language and Culture Centre at Hanoi University — operating since 2009, it teaches Thai to students and the public and is a living specimen of cultural exchange. Major Thai outlets including Matichon, Thairath and The Nation covered the visit extensively, focusing on the Hanoi programme and the 50th-anniversary significance; Thairath read it as an opportunity to "deepen relations between close and friendly neighbours." From a language classroom to media narratives, royal diplomacy translates high-level trust into a tangible people-to-people warmth.

## A dense year of high-level exchanges

The year 2026 has been unusually active in Vietnam–Thailand high-level contact. In May, To Lam visited Thailand and the two sides signed the 2026–2031 Action Plan for implementing their Comprehensive Strategic Partnership; in June, Prime Minister Anutin visited Vietnam for the third ASEAN Future Forum; in August, the President of Thailand's Parliament led a delegation to Hanoi. Within months, exchanges ran from the Party chief to the prime minister and the parliamentary speaker — a density and level rare even by regional standards. The Thai King's visit crowns this intense year of interaction, advancing political trust along multiple tracks at once: leadership, royalty and the people.

## A lesson between neighbours

Zooming out to Southeast Asia as a whole, the King's visit offers a template for handling neighbourhood relations. Vietnam and Thailand share historical closeness but also potential frictions — Mekong water resources, maritime claims — yet both chose to mark their anniversary and stage royal diplomacy by expanding cooperation and managing differences at the table. As great-power competition sharpens and some neighbouring ties fray, two peninsular powers choosing celebration over confrontation to mark their milestone carries demonstrative value. A half-century of neighbourhood, polished anew by a royal "moment in the spotlight."

## Further reading

**Comprehensive Strategic Partnership:** One of the highest tiers of diplomatic relations between states, covering political, security, economic and people-to-people cooperation across the board. Vietnam and Thailand elevated their ties to this level in May 2025.

**ASEAN:** The Association of Southeast Asian Nations, a ten-member regional bloc and the core mechanism for Southeast Asian cooperation and integration; both Thailand and Vietnam are early and pivotal members.
