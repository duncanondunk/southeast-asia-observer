# 第八届中国—东盟视听周：让屏幕成为"民相亲"的日常纽带

2026年9月28日至29日，广西在南宁召开第八届中国—东盟视听周新闻发布会。这场以"人工智能（AI）赋能、共创共享"为主题的活动，将于10月19日在广西北海开幕。它不只是一次文化展览，更是中国—东盟全面战略伙伴关系建立五周年主题年中的一项机制性安排。理解它，能帮我们看清中国面向东南亚的软实力落点，正从宏大叙事转向更细腻的"通民心"实践。

## 数字：95项合作、近700部互播节目

自2019年创办以来，中国—东盟视听周已孵化出青年主播创造营、译制展播、"同唱友谊歌"中越金曲音乐盛典等一批品牌活动，累计推出合作项目95项；近700部中国与东盟视听节目通过双方平台互译互播，走进彼此观众的客厅。《红楼梦》《漓江》《恋恋泰兰德》《越南四季》等作品，让两方面的故事、文化与生活方式，借由屏幕相互走近。数字背后，是一套涵盖青年交流、内容创作、节目译制、国际传播与产业合作的稳定活动体系。

## 人工智能：从内容到传播的"广西集成"

本届最突出的标签是"人工智能赋能"。官方表述为"北上广研发+广西集成+东盟应用"的发展路径——把一线城市的算法能力，在广西完成面向东盟的场景集成，再投放到区域市场。人工智能被嵌入微短剧的创作、译制配音与传播分发环节；"同唱友谊歌"活动还以人工智能实现双语歌曲共创。技术在这里不是噱头，而是合作的新引擎：它降低了跨境内容的生产与语言门槛，让广西从"通道"升级为"集成者"。

## 微短剧出海：把合作从展示推向市场

首届中国—东盟微短剧大赛是本届的创新点。它搭建了跨境征集、联合创作与项目推介的一体化平台，鼓励双方创作者携手打造"接地气、通民心"的跨境作品，并在开幕式揭晓结果。配套发布的《中国—东盟微短剧国际传播发展报告》，为获奖团队对接制作、传播、推广与版权交易平台。这意味着双方的视听合作，正从"交流展示"走向"市场对接"与"产业协同"——文化项目开始具备自我造血的商业逻辑。

## 青年主播与金曲：让"民相亲"长在自己人身上

青年是本届的另一个重心。2026中国—东盟青年主播创造营首次集齐东盟11国"全家福"，31名来自中国与东盟的青年主播分赴北京、南宁与斯里巴加湾市研学，以短视频讲述双方友好故事。与此同时，"同唱友谊歌"在越南社交平台发起"十大中越金曲"网络演唱，并于10月30日在河内举办音乐盛典。把叙事权交给年轻人、把共鸣点放在金曲与日常，是让"民相亲"真正扎根的有效路径。

## 中国的软实力，正在学会"说小事"

把上述线索连起来看，中国面向东盟的软实力实践，正在发生微妙转向。过去，文化外交常陷于宏大的概念宣介；而视听周的逻辑是：用可感的屏幕内容、可参与的青年项目、可传唱的金曲，把"命运共同体"从文件语言变成观众的日常经验。这种做法未必轰轰烈烈，却更经得起时间——当东盟观众在屏幕上看见彼此真实的生活，政治话语便让位于人情。人工智能的加入，则让这套打法有了可规模化的技术底座。

## 延伸阅读

**中国—东盟全面战略伙伴关系（China–ASEAN Comprehensive Strategic Partnership）**：2021年11月确立的中国与东盟最高层级合作框架，涵盖政治、经济、安全与人文等领域；本届视听周被列为该伙伴关系建立五周年主题年系列活动之一。

**微短剧（Micro-Drama）**：单集时长短、节奏快、适合移动端与社交传播的竖屏剧集，近年成为中国视听出海的新载体，也是本届跨境内容合作的重点赛道。

**青年主播创造营（Youth Anchor Camp）**：中国—东盟视听周旗下品牌活动，组织双方青年以短视频创作与城市研学讲述友好故事，本届首次实现东盟11国全员参与。

# The 8th China–ASEAN Audio-Visual Week: Screens as an Everyday Thread of People-to-People Bonds

On 28–29 September 2026, Guangxi held a press conference in Nanning for the 8th China–ASEAN Audio-Visual Week. Themed "AI Empowered, Co-created and Shared," the event will open on 19 October in Beihai, Guangxi. It is not merely a cultural showcase but an institutional arrangement within the commemorative programme for the fifth anniversary of the China–ASEAN Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. Understanding it helps us see where China's soft power in Southeast Asia is now landing — and reveals a quiet shift from grand narrative toward the finer practice of reaching people's hearts.

## The numbers: 95 programmes, nearly 700 titles exchanged

Since its launch in 2019, the China–ASEAN Audio-Visual Week has incubated flagship activities — the Youth Anchor Camp, the translation-and-broadcast programme and the "Sing Friendship Songs" China–Vietnam golden-song gala — and rolled out 95 cooperation projects in total. Nearly 700 Chinese and ASEAN audiovisual titles have been translated and aired across each other's platforms, entering viewers' living rooms. Works such as Dream of the Red Chamber, Li River, My Amazement of Thailand and Four Seasons of Vietnam have brought each side's stories, cultures and ways of life closer through the screen. Behind the numbers sits a stable architecture spanning youth exchange, content creation, programme translation, international communication and industrial cooperation.

## AI empowerment: from content to distribution, the "Guangxi integration"

The defining label of this edition is "AI empowerment." Officials frame a path of "R&D in first-tier cities, integration in Guangxi, application in ASEAN" — taking the algorithmic capacity of Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou, integrating it in Guangxi for ASEAN-facing scenarios, then releasing it into the regional market. AI is woven into micro-drama creation, dubbing and distribution; "Sing Friendship Songs" even uses AI for bilingual song co-creation. Technology here is no gimmick but a new engine of cooperation: it lowers the production and language thresholds of cross-border content and upgrades Guangxi from a "corridor" into an "integrator."

## Micro-dramas going out: from showcase to market

The inaugural China–ASEAN Micro-Drama Contest is this edition's innovation. It builds an integrated platform for cross-border solicitation, co-creation and project pitching, encouraging creators from both sides to produce grounded, people-friendly cross-border works, with results unveiled at the opening ceremony. A companion China–ASEAN Micro-Drama International Communication Development Report connects award-winning teams to production, distribution, promotion and copyright-trading platforms. This means the two sides' audiovisual cooperation is moving from "exchange and display" toward "market matching" and "industrial synergy" — cultural projects are beginning to acquire a self-sustaining commercial logic.

## Young anchors and golden songs: letting "people bonds" grow on local youth

Youth is the other centre of gravity this year. The 2026 China–ASEAN Youth Anchor Camp gathered, for the first time, a "family portrait" of all 11 ASEAN countries; 31 young anchors from China and ASEAN studied in Beijing, Nanning and Bandar Seri Begawan, telling stories of friendship through short videos. In parallel, "Sing Friendship Songs" launched a "Top 10 Sino-Vietnamese Golden Songs" online sing-along on Vietnamese social platforms, culminating in a gala in Hanoi on 30 October. Handing the narrative to young people and anchoring resonance in golden songs and daily life is the most effective way to root "people-to-people bonds" in place.

## China's soft power is learning to tell small stories

Connect the threads and a subtle turn in China's soft-power practice toward ASEAN comes into view. In the past, cultural diplomacy often stalled in grand conceptual promotion; the logic of the Audio-Visual Week is different — using tangible screen content, participatory youth projects and singable golden songs to turn "a community with a shared future" from document language into viewers' everyday experience. This approach may not be spectacular, but it endures: when ASEAN audiences see each other's real lives on screen, political rhetoric gives way to human feeling. The addition of AI gives this playbook a scalable technological base.

## Further reading

**China–ASEAN Comprehensive Strategic Partnership**: The highest-tier cooperation framework between China and ASEAN, established in November 2021, spanning politics, economy, security and people-to-people ties; this year's Audio-Visual Week is listed among the commemorative activities for its fifth anniversary.

**Micro-Drama**: Short, fast-paced, vertically filmed series suited to mobile and social distribution; in recent years a new vehicle for China's audiovisual going global and a key track in this edition's cross-border content cooperation.

**Youth Anchor Camp**: A flagship activity under the China–ASEAN Audio-Visual Week that organises young creators from both sides to tell friendship stories through short-video production and city study tours; this edition achieved full participation by all 11 ASEAN countries for the first time.
