# 侨心越山海：台风"沙德尔"后，海外莆商把善款汇向故土

## 一笔从南太平洋汇来的捐款

2026年9月10日，一笔6万元人民币的爱心款，从南太平洋岛国瓦努阿图汇向福建莆田市涵江区。捐款人郑玉鹏祖籍涵江区国欢镇新坡村，是一位身居海外的华侨。这已是他第二次为家乡的灾后恢复重建解囊。身在他乡、不忘桑梓，一笔笔跨越山海的汇款，承载着万里之外的游子对故土最深切的牵挂。当洪水退去、家园待建，这些汇自异国的款项，成了灾后重建最初、也最温暖的底气。

## 洪水里的不眠夜

这份牵挂，早在灾情发生之初就已涌动。9月3日以来，台风"沙德尔"带来的持续强降雨袭击涵江，洪水漫进村庄，也牵动了万里之外的侨心。5日深夜，远在马来西亚吉隆坡的侨胞马金福不停翻看手机——祖籍涵江区梧塘镇沁后村的他，在视频里看到洪水漫过家乡道路、救援人员蹚水转移群众。由于一度联系不上亲人，他和不少侨胞彻夜难眠。"后来，家乡通过侨联、海联会及时传来平安消息，我们悬着的心才放下来。"他说。

## "沙德尔"为何重创莆田

这场灾难的来势，超出许多人的预期。台风"沙德尔"的残余涡旋与季风气流叠加，自8月31日至9月4日在福建沿海制造历史罕见的极端强降雨。其中，仙游县一举突破1957年有完整观测以来的历史纪录；涵江等侨乡多处被淹，道路中断、民房进水，损失较重。对习惯了台风季的沿海百姓而言，这一次的雨量之猛、范围之广，仍属少见。灾情初现，便立刻转化为对在外乡亲的情感冲击——因为莆田，本就是一座"侨"味极浓的城市。

## 侨乡的基因

莆田是中国著名的侨乡，而涵江更是其中的代表。数据显示，涵江籍海外华侨华人分布在全球87个国家和地区。从东南亚的新加坡、马来西亚，到南美的苏里南、圭亚那，再到欧洲的英国、大洋洲的瓦努阿图，几乎每个有海水的地方，都有莆田人的身影。他们多以五金、超市、贸易起家，在异国他乡扎下根，却始终在族谱与方言里守着"莆田"二字。正是这种绵长的侨乡基因，让一场家乡的洪水，能在一夜之间唤醒全球莆商的集体记忆。

## 一笔笔汇向故土

灾情发生后，涵江区侨联、海外联谊会迅速发出"同心共护家园"捐赠倡议，得到海内外乡亲的踊跃响应。新加坡侨胞张志建第一时间捐赠53万元，并紧急筹集摩托车、消杀设备、消毒药剂等物资驰援白塘镇；新加坡莆仙同乡联合会会长方志忠通过企业向江口镇捐赠53万元；香港莆仙同乡联合会会长林平基捐赠15万元，该会累计捐赠134.1万元；香港同胞、涵江区侨联名誉主席黄光辉捐赠26万元。截至10日晚，港澳同胞、海外侨胞等已捐赠救灾资金450多万元，并筹集大批防汛排涝、卫生消杀物资。

## 从东南亚到南美、欧洲

善款的来源，画出了一幅全球莆商的地图。9月6日，新加坡莆仙同乡联合会向莆田市捐赠300万元人民币；在马来西亚、印尼，侨领陈财基、陈永辉等纷纷以个人与企业名义捐款驰援江口镇。跨过半个地球，南美洲的苏里南、圭亚那两国游洋籍侨胞截至9月8日12时已捐款30万余元——苏里南福建同乡会会长林金禄率先捐出3万元，商会成员以美元、欧元、人民币多种币种表达爱心。远在欧洲，英国福莆仙同乡会于9月15日捐赠21.146万元，定向支援涵江、江口、华亭等多地。山海隔不断乡情，危难显赤子初心。

## 侨联搭起的连心桥

在这场跨越国界的爱心接力中，侨联与海外联谊会扮演了"连心桥"的角色。它们一面及时向外发布家乡平安消息，安抚了无数个像马金福一样彻夜难眠的侨胞；一面发出捐赠倡议，把分散在全球的善念汇聚成有序的支援。从倡议、对接、接收，到物资调配与公示，这条纽带让"想帮忙"的侨胞找得到门路、看得到去向。也正是在这一机制下，企业力量同步汇入：信和集团及黄廷方慈善基金捐赠200万元，福建本土企业星宸科技定向捐赠200万元支援华亭镇，莆田市河南商会、厦门及义乌莆田商会也火速驰援。

## 桑梓情为何历久弥新

从瓦努阿图的6万元，到全球莆商的数百万元，汇向莆田的不仅是一笔笔善款，更是海外华侨华人与故土之间历久弥新的情感契约。对许多旅居海外的莆商而言，无论走得多远、事业做多大，"莆田"始终是方言里最柔软的那部分。当家乡遭遇急难，他们挺身而出、共克时艰，诠释的正是"侨心系故土、大爱助桑梓"的赤子情怀。一场台风带来伤痛，也照见了一种跨越山海、连接代际的凝聚力——这或许就是侨乡最深沉的力量。

## 延伸阅读

**侨乡（Qiaoxiang）**：指海外华侨华人集中、侨眷众多的家乡地区，多以血缘、地缘与方言为纽带。福建莆田、广东潮汕、浙江青田等均为著名侨乡。

**侨联（Overseas Chinese Federation）**：中华全国归国华侨联合会及其地方组织的简称，负责联系海外侨胞与归侨侨眷，在赈灾、公益与文化交流中常充当海内外沟通的桥梁。

# Overseas Chinese Aid Flows Home: After Typhoon Shadel, Putian Diaspora Sends Funds Across the Seas

## A donation wired from the South Pacific

On 10 September 2026, a 60,000-yuan contribution was wired from the South Pacific island nation of Vanuatu to Hanjiang district in Putian, Fujian. The donor, Zheng Yupeng, traces his ancestry to Xinpo village in Guohuan town, Hanjiang — he is an overseas Chinese living abroad. This was already his second donation for his hometown's post-disaster recovery. Far from home yet never forgetting the native soil, remittances like his carry the deepest concern of compatriots thousands of miles away. As the floodwaters receded and homes awaited rebuilding, these funds sent from foreign shores became the first and warmest cushion for reconstruction.

## Sleepless nights in the flood

The concern surged from the very onset of the disaster. From 3 September, the sustained heavy rain brought by Typhoon Shadel struck Hanjiang; water flooded villages and tugged at the hearts of overseas kin. On the night of the 5th, Ma Jinfu, an expatriate in Kuala Lumpur whose ancestral home is Qinhuo village in Hutong town, kept refreshing his phone. In videos he saw water overrunning hometown roads as rescuers waded through to evacuate residents. Unable to reach his family for a time, he and many others stayed up all night. "Later, the homeland sent word of safety through the Overseas Chinese federation and the friendship association, and only then did our hearts settle," he said.

## Why Shadel hit Putian so hard

The force of the calamity exceeded many expectations. The remnant vortex of Typhoon Shadel,叠加 with monsoon airflow, produced historically rare extreme rainfall along the Fujian coast from 31 August to 4 September. Xianyou county broke its observational record dating back to 1957; Hanjiang and other qiaoxiang towns were broadly inundated, with roads cut and homes flooded, causing heavy losses. For coastal residents accustomed to typhoon seasons, the intensity and spread of this rainfall were still unusual. As the disaster emerged, it instantly became an emotional shock for compatriots abroad — because Putian is, above all, a city steeped in "qiao," the diaspora.

## The DNA of a qiaoxiang

Putian is a famed hometown of overseas Chinese in China, and Hanjiang is among its emblems. Data show that Hanjiang-origin overseas Chinese are scattered across 87 countries and territories. From Singapore and Malaysia in Southeast Asia to Suriname and Guyana in South America, to Britain in Europe and Vanuatu in Oceania, wherever there is seawater, there are people from Putian. Many started in hardware, supermarkets and trade, putting down roots abroad while keeping "Putian" alive in their genealogies and dialect. It is this long qiaoxiang lineage that lets a single hometown flood awaken the collective memory of Putian merchants worldwide overnight.

## Funds flowing home

Once the disaster struck, Hanjiang's Overseas Chinese federation and overseas friendship association swiftly issued a "protect our home together" donation appeal, drawing an enthusiastic response from compatriots at home and abroad. Singaporean compatriot Zhang Zhijian donated 530,000 yuan immediately and urgently procured motorcycles, disinfection equipment and sanitizers for Baitang town; Fang Zhizhong, head of the Singapore Putian-Hinghwa association, gave 530,000 yuan through his firm to Jiangkou town; Lin Pingji, chairman of the Hong Kong Putian-Hinghwa association, donated 150,000 yuan, while the association as a whole gave 1,341,000 yuan; Wong Kwong-fai, a Hong Kong compatriot and honorary chairman of the Hanjiang federation, contributed 260,000 yuan. By the evening of the 10th, compatriots from Hong Kong, Macau and overseas had donated over 4.5 million yuan, plus large quantities of flood-drainage and sanitation supplies.

## From Southeast Asia to South America and Europe

The origins of the aid sketch a map of global Putian merchants. On 6 September, the Singapore Putian-Hinghwa association donated 3 million yuan to the city; in Malaysia and Indonesia, leaders such as Chen Caiji and Chen Yonghui gave personally and through enterprises for Jiangkou town. Half a world away, by 12:00 on 8 September, Putian-origin compatriots in Suriname and Guyana had donated over 300,000 yuan — Lin Jinlu, head of the Suriname Fujian association, led with 30,000 yuan, and members contributed in US dollars, euros and RMB. In Europe, the UK Fukien-Putian-Hinghwa association gave 211,460 yuan on 15 September, earmarked for Hanjiang, Jiangkou, Huating and other localities. Mountains and seas cannot sever kinship; crisis reveals the migrant's original heart.

## The bridge built by the federation

In this cross-border relay of goodwill, the Overseas Chinese federation and friendship association played the role of a "bridge of hearts." On one side they promptly relayed news of safety from the homeland, soothing countless compatriots like Ma who had stayed awake all night; on the other they issued the donation appeal, channelling scattered goodwill worldwide into orderly support. From appeal and matching to receipt and disclosure, this link let those who "wanted to help" find the door and see where their money went. It was through this mechanism that corporate strength joined in: Sino Group and the Henry Fok–style charity fund gave 2 million yuan, local Fujian firm SigmaStar donated 2 million yuan earmarked for Huating town, and the Henan, Xiamen and Yiwu Putian chambers of commerce rushed aid as well.

## Why homeland affection endures

From the 60,000 yuan out of Vanuatu to the millions from Putian merchants worldwide, what flows to Putian is not merely money but an emotional covenant between overseas Chinese and their native soil that endures across generations. For many Putian merchants abroad, no matter how far they roam or how large their enterprises grow, "Putian" remains the softest syllable in their dialect. When the homeland meets sudden hardship, they step forward to weather it together — embodying the migrant's devotion of "heart bound to the soil, great love aiding the native place." A typhoon brings wounds, yet also illuminates a cohesion that crosses mountains and seas and binds the generations — perhaps the deepest strength of a qiaoxiang.

## Further reading

**Qiaoxiang (Hometown of Overseas Chinese):** A home region with a dense concentration of overseas Chinese and their relatives, bound chiefly by blood, place of origin and dialect. Fujian's Putian, Guangdong's Teochew and Zhejiang's Qingtian are among the most famous qiaoxiang.

**Overseas Chinese Federation:** The All-China Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese and its local branches, which liaise with overseas compatriots and returned families and often serve as a bridge between homeland and diaspora in disaster relief, charity and cultural exchange.
