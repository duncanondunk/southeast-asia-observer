# 八十一年后的白菊：吉隆坡两度公祭南侨机工，谁在记住那段归国路

2026年，吉隆坡（Kuala Lumpur）两度举办公祭南侨机工典礼。7月5日，由隆雪华堂主办的典礼在广东义山马来亚南侨机工纪念公园举行；8月15日，吉隆坡广东义山再办"815 公祭"，呼应世界反法西斯战争胜利 81周年。两场仪式合计逾百个社团、上千名市民与青年学生到场，纪念碑前的白菊一年比一年密。

## 一座纪念碑，两个年份

1947年落成的雪兰莪华侨机工回国抗战殉难纪念碑，是全球首座专门纪念南侨机工援华抗战的丰碑。它立在吉隆坡市中心一片被称为"城市绿肺"的公共墓园里，旁边是马来亚二战人民蒙难纪念总碑。今年公祭由隆雪华堂牵头，共有 84家华人社团参与联办与协办，是 2021年恢复举办以来参与华团最多的一届；吉隆坡四所独立中学约 60名师生也列队诵读祭文。

## 三千二百人，一条"死亡公路"

故事要回到 1939年。逾 3200名南洋华侨青年响应爱国侨领陈嘉庚的号召，告别椰风蕉雨的故乡，赴华投身抗战。他们在被后世称作"死亡公路"的滇缅公路上抢运战略军需，在日军轰炸、疟疾与塌方之间往返。资料显示，其中逾千名机工再没能回来。他们打通的，是支撑抗战的关键生命线。

## 从"回望"到"活在当下"

与往年不同，今年的纪念明显更"年轻"。主办者提议用人工智能创新传播形式，让年轻一代认识这段历史；本地华校同步启动"机工口述史进课堂"计划。8月15日的配套活动还发布了《南侨机工人物汇编（第一册）》，并举办"马来西亚（Malaysia）与东南亚（Southeast Asia）二战记忆与纪念碑国际研讨会"。记忆正在从碑文走向课堂、从追思走向研究。

## 当祖父的名字第一次被念出

最动人的一幕发生在 8月15日。首相署（联邦直辖区）部长杨巧双致辞时说，她的祖父杨妈富正是当年奔赴滇缅前线的南侨机工，而她出生前祖父便已离世，只能从祖母口中拼凑零星往事。"今天是我第一次从历史文献里看到祖父的生平。"她数度哽咽，并宣布拨款 5万林吉特（约 8.25万元人民币）支持广东义山文史工作。

## 尾声：记忆是一种代际的托付

中国驻马来西亚大使在致辞中提醒，须警惕美化侵略、复活军国主义的图谋，各国应携手维护二战胜利成果。但对许多到场家庭而言，公祭的意义更朴素：把祖辈"舍生忘死"四个字，原样交到下一代手里。当白菊被一阵细雨打湿，碑前的年轻人低头默哀——这条连接故土与散居者的线，并未因年代久远而断。

## 延伸阅读

- **隆雪华堂（Kuala Lumpur and Selangor Chinese Assembly Hall）**：吉隆坡暨雪兰莪中华大会堂，近年南侨机工公祭的主要主办方。

# White Chrysanthemums Eighty-One Years On: Kuala Lumpur Mourns the Nanyang Mechanics — and Asks Who Remembers

In 2026, Kuala Lumpur held two memorial ceremonies for the Nanyang Mechanics. On 5 July, the Kuala Lumpur and Selangor Chinese Assembly Hall (KLSCAH) hosted a ceremony at the Malayan Nanyang Mechanics Memorial Park in Kwong Tong Cemetery; on 15 August, the cemetery held its "815" memorial marking the 81st anniversary of the end of the Second World War. Together the events drew over a hundred organisations and more than a thousand citizens and students, laying white chrysanthemums ever more thickly at the foot of the monument.

## One Monument, Two Dates

Erected in 1947, the Selangor Memorial to the Fallen Nanyang Mechanics is the world's first monument dedicated to their cause. It stands in a central Kuala Lumpur cemetery known as the city's "green lung," beside the Malayan Memorial to WWII Civilian Victims. This year's ceremony, led by KLSCAH, involved 84 Chinese associations — the most since the rite was revived in 2021 — with some 60 students from four independent Chinese schools reciting the eulogy.

## Three Thousand Two Hundred, One "Road of Death"

The story reaches back to 1939. More than 3,200 young overseas Chinese from across Nanyang answered the call of patriot Tan Kah Kee, leaving their island homes to serve in China's war effort. They hauled strategic supplies along the Burma Road — later called the "Road of Death" — under Japanese bombing, malaria and landslides. Over a thousand never returned. What they kept open was a lifeline of the resistance.

## From Looking Back to Living Memory

This year's observances felt younger. Organisers proposed using artificial intelligence to refresh the storytelling for a new generation, while local Chinese schools launched an "oral history in the classroom" programme. On 15 August, the accompanying events saw the release of the first volume of *Nanyang Mechanics: Collected Biographies* and an international seminar on Southeast Asia's WWII memory and monuments. Memory is moving from inscription to classroom, from mourning to research.

## When a Grandfather's Name Was Read Aloud

The most moving moment came on 15 August. Minister in the Prime Minister's Department (Federal Territories) Yang Soo Siu said her grandfather, Yeung Ma Foo, had been one of the mechanics, but had died before she was born; she had known him only through her grandmother's fragments. "Today is the first time I have seen his life in the historical record," she said, tears welling, and pledged 50,000 ringgit (roughly 82,500 RMB, about US$11,500) to support the cemetery's archival work.

## Coda: Memory as a Generational Trust

China's ambassador to Malaysia warned against any attempt to glorify aggression or revive militarism, urging all nations to defend the postwar order. But for many families present, the meaning was simpler: to pass the words "risk life and limb" from one generation to the next, intact. As a light rain dampened the chrysanthemums, young people bowed their heads — the thread linking homeland and diaspora unbroken by the years.

## Further reading

- **Nanyang Mechanics (南洋机工)**: The overseas Chinese youth who answered Tan Kah Kee's 1939 call to haul supplies in China; over 3,200 joined and more than 1,000 perished.
- **KLSCAH (隆雪华堂)**: The Kuala Lumpur and Selangor Chinese Assembly Hall, lead organiser of the revived memorial since 2021.
- **Burma Road (滇缅公路)**: The Yunnan–Burma supply line of the war, notorious for its peril and casualties, the "Road of Death."
