# 东盟—中国自贸区3.0：时间表与悬念

东盟—中国自由贸易区 3.0 版谈判在 2024 年完成实质性磋商，2025 年签署框架，2026 年进入落地执行的关键窗口。作为全球最大发展中国家间的自贸安排，3.0 版试图在 2.0 版（2015）的降税成果之上，把规则拓展到数字经济、绿色经济与供应链互联互通。但落地的节奏与成色，仍挂着几个悬念。

## 3.0 改了什么：从"降税"到"定规则"

如果说 1.0/2.0 版的主线是关税减让与货物自由化，3.0 版的主线则是"规则现代化"。其新增章节覆盖数字经济、绿色经济、竞争政策、中小企业与供应链韧性。这意味着双方合作从"把东西卖过来"，升级到"把规则立起来"——对东盟而言，这是借中国大市场倒逼自身制度升级的机会。

## 时间表的硬约束

3.0 版并非一签即生效。它需各成员国完成国内审批，再进入分步实施。2026 年的看点，是主要经济体能否按期完成批准，以及首批"早期收获"条款能否先行落地。时间表的硬约束在于：东盟十国内部审批节奏不一，任一环节的拖沓都会拉慢整体生效。

## 悬念一：数字规则的边界

数字经济章节是 3.0 的最大亮点，也是最大分歧点。数据跨境流动、本地化要求、数字税——这些既是发展红利，也是监管主权。东盟各国数字治理水平参差，能否在"便利"与"安全"间达成可操作的共识，决定该章节是实质突破还是原则宣示。

## 悬念二：绿色经济的"标准之争"

绿色经济章节涉及新能源、碳市场与可持续贸易。但"绿色"由谁定义、标准如何互认，是潜在摩擦点。对东盟出口导向型经济体而言，若绿色门槛被单方面抬高，可能抵消关税红利。标准之争，本质是规则话语权的争夺。

## 值得观察的几点

其一，国内审批的完成度与"早期收获"的落地范围。其二，数字经济章节的过渡期安排，能否照顾后进成员。其三，3.0 与 RCEP、CPTPP 的叠加效应——多重自贸网络会否让企业"规则过载"。

归根结底，东盟—中国自贸区 3.0，是一份面向未来的规则清单，而非一张即时的降税支票。它的价值不在签署那一刻，而在未来数年能否被认真执行。悬念未消之际，真正的较量，是规则落地时的诚意与能力。

## 延伸阅读

**自贸协定 3.0（ACFTA 3.0）**：东盟—中国自由贸易区升级版，在 2.0 降税基础上拓展数字经济、绿色经济、供应链等规则章节，2024 完成磋商。

**早期收获（Early Harvest）**：自贸谈判中先行实施的部分降税或开放条款，意在让部分成果早于全面协定生效。

**数字经济章节（Digital Economy Chapter）**：协定中专涉数据流动、数字贸易与网络安全等规则的模块，是 3.0 版的核心新增内容。

# ASEAN–China FTA 3.0: The Timeline and the Suspense

The ASEAN–China Free Trade Area (ACFTA) 3.0 concluded substantive talks in 2024, signed its framework in 2025 and entered a critical window for implementation in 2026. As the largest FTA between developing economies, version 3.0 tries to extend rules from the tariff cuts of 2.0 (2015) to the digital economy, green economy and supply-chain connectivity. But the pace and quality of implementation still hang on several suspense points.

## What 3.0 changes: from 'cutting tariffs' to 'setting rules'

If versions 1.0/2.0 were about tariff reduction and goods liberalisation, 3.0 is about 'rule modernisation'. Its new chapters cover digital economy, green economy, competition policy, SMEs and supply-chain resilience. This upgrades cooperation from 'selling goods across' to 'setting rules up' — for ASEAN, a chance to leverage the China market to force its own institutional upgrade.

## The hard constraints of the timeline

Version 3.0 does not take effect upon signing. It needs each member's domestic ratification, then phased implementation. The 2026 watch-point is whether major economies complete ratification on schedule and whether the first 'early harvest' clauses land first. The hard constraint: ASEAN's ten members approve at uneven speeds, and any lag drags the whole entry into force.

## Suspense one: the boundaries of digital rules

The digital-economy chapter is 3.0's biggest highlight and its biggest fault line. Cross-border data flows, localisation requirements, digital taxes — these are both development dividends and regulatory sovereignty. ASEAN members vary in digital governance; whether they reach an operable consensus between 'convenience' and 'security' decides whether the chapter is a real breakthrough or a principled statement.

## Suspense two: the 'standards contest' in green economy

The green-economy chapter touches new energy, carbon markets and sustainable trade. But who defines 'green' and how standards mutualise is a friction point. For ASEAN's export-oriented economies, unilaterally raised green thresholds could offset tariff dividends. The standards contest is essentially a fight for rule-setting power.

## A few things to watch

First, the completion of domestic ratification and the scope of 'early harvest' landing. Second, transition arrangements in the digital chapter that accommodate late members. Third, the stacking effect of 3.0 with RCEP and CPTPP — whether multiple FTA networks overload enterprises with 'rule fatigue'.

In the end, ACFTA 3.0 is a future-facing rule list, not an instant tariff-cut cheque. Its value lies not in the signing moment but in whether it is seriously executed over coming years. With suspense unresolved, the real contest is the sincerity and capacity at the moment of implementation.

## Further reading

**ACFTA 3.0**: The upgraded ASEAN–China Free Trade Area, extending 2.0's tariff cuts with rule chapters on digital economy, green economy and supply chains; substantive talks concluded in 2024.

**Early Harvest**: Portions of tariff cuts or liberalisation implemented ahead of the full agreement, letting some results take effect earlier.

**Digital Economy Chapter**: The module in an agreement specifically covering data flows, digital trade and cyber-security rules; a core new addition in 3.0.
