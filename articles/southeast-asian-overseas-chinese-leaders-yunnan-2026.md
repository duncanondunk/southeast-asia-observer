# 侨见中国商机：28名东南亚侨领赴滇，寻找双向开放的接口

2026年7月15日，昆明（Kunming）的"2026年海外侨领相约彩云南研习活动"正式启动。28名来自缅甸（Myanmar）、泰国（Thailand）、老挝（Laos）的海外侨领，将在云南（Yunnan）展开为期七天的行程。活动由云南省人民政府侨务办公室主办，于7月14日至20日举行。他们聆听专题讲座，走进昆明规划馆与COP15生物多样性（biodiversity）室外展示基地，随后前往玉溪（Yuxi）探访产业、体验非遗。

## 一程云南，三重信号

云南地处中国西南边陲，正从开放的"末梢"转向面向南亚东南亚的"前沿"。这场研习并非观光，而是把"侨"的桥梁作用落到具体项目上。对侨领而言，云南不再只是故土记忆里的省份，更是连通中国与东盟（ASEAN）市场的接口。

## 侨资的"西南通道"

泰国云南会馆青年会总干事雷锦璨祖籍云南腾冲，长年在泰国深耕旅游业。此行中的智能化工厂令他印象深刻，他希望借文体与研学凝聚泰国华裔青年，也计划多带下一代回云南走走。缅籍华人吕子态长期从事华文教育，首次到访玉溪便对工业遗产的保护利用模式称赞有加。缅甸第四代华人杨荣德经营食品与电器，其瓜子、电器多从中国进口，仅瓜子年销量就超300吨——他此行想挖掘更多云南优质产品，丰富自身品类。这些个案说明，侨资与侨企早已深度嵌入中国—东盟供应链。

## 从"引资"到"引智引链"

玉溪地处滇中腹地，产业基础坚实，已形成烟草、矿冶、新能源电池全产业链及生物医药等集群，并重点推出新能源电池、现代物流、生物医药等十大招商项目。当晚的交流会上，当地部门推介营商环境与投资机会。近年来，云南依托南博会、东盟华商会等平台，常态化对接侨商，推动"侨资入滇"。

## 侨青接棒，代际传承

多位侨领不约而同提到，要以好项目激发华裔新生代的热情，为其搭建展示才华的平台，增强对祖（籍）国的文化认同。雷锦璨说，应多办这类"零距离"交流，通过双向信息互通为各方创造合作可能。让新生代成为中外友谊的新生力量，正是侨务工作面向未来的落点。

## 小切口里的大命题

28人、七天，看似微小，却折射出区域合作的宏大命题。中老铁路（China–Laos Railway）的通道效能、区域全面经济伙伴关系协定（RCEP）的红利、云南作为面向南亚东南亚辐射中心的定位，正在重塑西南边疆的经济地理。当侨领既是投资者、也是文化使者，他们在双向开放中的"超级联系人"价值，愈发清晰。

# Seeing China's Opportunities: 28 Southeast Asian Overseas Chinese Leaders Tour Yunnan in Search of Two-Way Opening

On 15 July 2026, the "2026 Overseas Chinese Leaders' Yunnan Study Tour" officially opened in Kunming. Twenty-eight leaders from the overseas Chinese communities of Myanmar, Thailand and Laos embarked on a seven-day itinerary across Yunnan. Organised by the Overseas Chinese Affairs Office of the Yunnan Provincial Government and running from 14 to 20 July, the programme paired lectures with site visits — from the Kunming Planning Exhibition Hall to the COP15 outdoor biodiversity showcase — before moving on to Yuxi for industry tours and intangible-cultural-heritage experiences.

## A Yunnan Trip, Three Signals

Yunnan, on China's southwestern frontier, is shifting from the "end of the line" to the "frontier" facing South and Southeast Asia. This study tour was no sightseeing junket; it was about turning the diaspora's bridging role into concrete projects. For these leaders, Yunnan is no longer merely a province in ancestral memory but an interface linking China with the ASEAN market.

## The "Southwestern Corridor" of Diaspora Capital

Lei Jinzhan, general secretary of the youth arm of the Thailand Yunnan Association, was struck by the smart factories on the route and hopes to use sport, culture and study trips to rally Thai ethnic-Chinese youth — and to bring the next generation back to Yunnan. U Zih Tui, a Myanmar Chinese long engaged in Chinese-language education, praised Yuxi's model of preserving industrial heritage. Yang Rongde, a fourth-generation Myanmar Chinese in the food and appliances trade, imports most of his goods from China and sells over 300 tonnes of sunflower seeds a year alone; he came looking for more quality Yunnan products to widen his range. Together, these cases show how diaspora capital and enterprises are already woven into the China–ASEAN supply chain.

## From "Attracting Capital" to "Attracting Talent and Chains"

Yuxi, in central Yunnan, boasts a solid industrial base — tobacco, mining, full new-energy-battery and biopharma clusters — and is promoting ten flagship investment projects in new-energy batteries, modern logistics and biomedicine. At an evening forum, local departments pitched the business environment and opportunities. In recent years Yunnan has used platforms such as the China–South Asia Expo and the ASEAN Chinese Business Conference to engage diaspora investors on a recurring basis.

## The Next Generation Takes the Baton

Several leaders stressed the need to energise ethnic-Chinese youth with good projects, build stages for them to shine, and deepen their cultural identification with the ancestral country. Lei said such "zero-distance" exchanges should be institutionalised, creating possibilities for all sides through two-way information flows. Making the young generation a fresh force for China–world friendship is precisely where diaspora affairs meet the future.

## A Grand Theme in a Small Cut

Twenty-eight people, seven days — modest in scale, yet revealing of a grand regional story. The China–Laos Railway's corridor effect, the dividends of the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP), and Yunnan's positioning as a pivot for South and Southeast Asia are redrawing the economic geography of the southwestern frontier. As leaders act simultaneously as investors and cultural envoys, their value as "super-connectors" in two-way opening grows ever clearer.
