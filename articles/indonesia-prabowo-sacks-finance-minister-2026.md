# 普拉博沃闪电撤换财长：印尼财政的"稳"与"进"

## 一场毫无预兆的换帅

2026年9月14日，印尼总统普拉博沃·苏比安托在雅加达国家宫主持宣誓仪式，任命苏阿希勒·纳扎拉（Suahasil Nazara）为新任财政部长，接替普尔巴亚·尤迪·萨德瓦（Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa）。这道以第97P号总统令发布的任免，事前几乎无人预料——苏阿希勒本人也是当天上午才被告知，下午即宣誓就职；而被免职的普尔巴亚，当天上午还在国会参加听证会，中途被一通来自国务秘书的电话叫走，直到晚些时候才知道自己已被替换。一场影响东南亚最大经济体财政走向的换帅，就这样在数小时内完成。

## 被换下的 Purbaya

普尔巴亚2025年9月8日接替长期财长英卓华（Sri Mulyani Indrawati）上任，此前是主导存款保险机构的低调技术官僚。但上任一年间，他以直率、不加修饰的行事风格引发争议：2026年4月，他提出对通过马六甲海峡的船只征收通行费，次日即被外交部长苏吉奥诺否认，指其不符合国际法；他还在财政部内推动大规模人事重组，数百名官员被调岗，其中包括约50名司局级官员；此外，他与主权财富基金达南塔拉在利润使用问题上公开分歧。媒体与分析人士认为，这些正是他"下课"的可能导火索。

## 新财长的"技术官僚"底色

接任者苏阿希勒绝非生手。他是印尼大学经济学教授，1994年获印尼大学经济学学士，1997年获康奈尔大学理学硕士，2003年获伊利诺伊大学厄巴纳-香槟分校经济学博士。2016至2019年，他出任财政部财政政策署（BKF）署长；2019年起任财政部副部长，历经佐科政府，并在普拉博沃政府中继续担任副部长，直至此次扶正。这意味着，他熟悉预算如何编制、税收如何征收、融资如何安排、财政与货币政策如何互动——他不需要时间"学习"这台机器如何运转。他由此成为普拉博沃任内的第三位财长。

## 汇率与赤字的两本账

换帅的背景，是持续累积的市场忧虑。普尔巴亚任内，印尼盾显著走弱；财政赤字则持续扩大——政府7月曾表示，2026年赤字可能扩大至国内生产总值（GDP）的2.85%，高于原先预测的2.68%，逼近3%的法定上限。更令投资者不安的是，惠誉（Fitch）与穆迪（Moody's）今年先后将印尼的信用评级展望由"稳定"下调至"负面"，理由正是政策不确定性与财政支出计划。评级机构的态度，直接打击了这个东南亚最大经济体的市场情绪。

## 2027预算的走钢丝

换帅发生在一个关键节点：政府正在编制2027年国家预算。普拉博沃自8月14日起在国会反复强调两个要求——既要更高的经济增长，又要维护财政健康。2027年的目标颇具张力：经济增长6%，赤字却要降至GDP的2.40%（低于2026年的2.68%）；国家支出计划约4097.2万亿印尼盾，收入约3426万亿印尼盾。用苏阿希勒的话说，这意味着每一盾支出都要产生乘数效应——不是简单削减开支，而是让有限的资金产出最大效益。

## 总统的指令：健康且可信

宣誓后，苏阿希勒对媒体转述了总统的明确指令："守护国家财政——既要保持健康，也要维护可信度。"他强调，作为国家财政的核心，预算必须健全，才能支撑政府的优先项目；同时必须可信，才能稳住公众与市场的信心。"预算必须值得信赖。"他承诺，将延续财政部已有的工作基础，在促进增长的财政政策与可持续的债务、赤字管理之间取得平衡。这一表态，被市场解读为对前任"激进扩张"路线的回调。

## 市场与国会的初步反应

初步反应偏正面。新加坡 Aletheia Capital 的东盟专家安格斯·麦金托什（Angus Mackintosh）认为，苏阿希勒的任命可能带来更稳定、更可预测的政策，其技术官僚背景与在英卓华手下历练的经历，是"强化稳定性的一步"。雅加达国立发展大学的公共政策分析人士艾哈迈德·努尔·希达亚特（Achmad Nur Hidayat）则指出，苏阿希勒"从内部懂得预算、税收、融资、补贴与财政治理"。国会第十一委员会副主席法乌齐·阿姆罗（Fauzi Amro）也公开支持这一任命，称其为"以解决问题为导向的人选"。

## 印尼财政的悬念

挑战清单并不轻松。苏阿希勒需要在前任留下的未竟任务上继续推进——优化税收、关税与消费税收入，扩大非税国家收入（PNBP），理顺中央与地方的财政转移支付；同时在全球压力与国内经济挑战中，兑现6%的增长目标而不突破赤字红线。有分析人士指出，他的风格比前任更为内敛，能否在普拉博沃的强势议程与市场的审慎期待之间找到平衡，将决定印尼财政的信用走向。一次仓促的换帅，把印尼经济最重要的两个目标——增长与稳健——同时压在了这位新财长的肩上。

## 延伸阅读

**达南塔拉（Danantara）**：印尼主权财富基金，统管多家国有企业股权，2025年成立，在财政与国资配置中角色吃重；其与财政部在利润使用等问题上的权责边界，是此次换帅风波的关键背景之一。

**国家预算（APBN）**：印尼年度国家预算案，宪法规定财政赤字不得超过国内生产总值的3%，是政府施政与宏观经济稳定的核心工具。

# Prabowo's Sudden Finance Minister Sacking: Indonesia's Fiscal Tightrope

## A reshuffle with no warning

On 14 September 2026, Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto swore in Suahasil Nazara as finance minister at the State Palace in Jakarta, replacing Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa. Issued under Presidential Decree No. 97P of 2026, the change caught almost everyone off guard — Suahasil himself was told only that morning and inaugurated that afternoon, while the ousted Purbaya was attending a parliamentary hearing when a call from the State Secretary pulled him away, learning of his replacement only later. A shake-up that will shape the fiscal course of Southeast Asia's largest economy was completed within hours.

## The minister who was replaced

Purbaya took office on 8 September 2025, succeeding long-serving finance minister Sri Mulyani Indrawati, after a low-profile career leading a deposit-insurance agency. In a single year, his blunt, unfiltered style stirred controversy: in April 2026 he floated a levy on ships passing through the Malacca Strait, denied a day later by Foreign Minister Sugiono as inconsistent with international law; he pushed a sweeping restructuring inside the ministry, reassigning hundreds of officials including some 50 at director level; and he publicly clashed with the sovereign wealth fund Danantara over the use of its profits. Analysts and media see these as the likely triggers for his exit.

## The new minister's technocratic pedigree

His successor is no novice. Suahasil is an economics professor at the University of Indonesia — a bachelor's there in 1994, a master's from Cornell in 1997 and a doctorate from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2003. He headed the ministry's Fiscal Policy Agency (BKF) from 2016 to 2019, then served as deputy finance minister from 2019 under Jokowi, continuing as deputy under Prabowo until his promotion. He already knows how budgets are drafted, revenue collected, financing arranged and fiscal policy interacts with monetary policy — no time is needed to "learn" the machine. He becomes Prabowo's third finance minister.

## Two ledgers: the rupiah and the deficit

The backdrop is mounting market anxiety. The rupiah weakened markedly during Purbaya's tenure, and the fiscal deficit kept widening — in July the government said it could reach 2.85 per cent of GDP this year, above its original 2.68 per cent forecast and close to the legal 3 per cent ceiling. More troubling for investors, Fitch and Moody's both cut Indonesia's credit outlook from stable to negative this year, citing policy uncertainty and spending plans. The agencies' stance has directly dented sentiment in Southeast Asia's biggest economy.

## The 2027 budget on a tightrope

The sacking lands at a critical moment: the government is drafting the 2027 budget. Since 14 August, Prabowo has pressed parliament with two demands at once — higher growth and fiscal health. The 2027 targets are deliberately tense: 6 per cent growth with the deficit falling to 2.40 per cent of GDP (from 2.68 per cent in 2026), on planned spending of about Rp4,097.2 trillion against Rp3,426 trillion in revenue. In Suahasil's phrasing, every rupiah must generate a multiplier — not simply cutting spending, but extracting maximum value from limited funds.

## The President's instruction: healthy and credible

After the swearing-in, Suahasil relayed the President's explicit instruction: safeguard state finances — keeping them healthy and upholding their credibility. As the core of state finance, he stressed, the budget must be sound to fund the government's priority programmes, and credible to hold public and market confidence. "The budget must be trustworthy," he said, pledging to build on the ministry's existing foundation and balance growth-oriented fiscal policy against sustainable debt and deficit management — a stance markets read as a correction of his predecessor's expansionary bent.

## First reactions from markets and parliament

Initial reactions leaned positive. Angus Mackintosh, an ASEAN specialist at Singapore-based Aletheia Capital, said Suahasil's appointment could bring more stable and predictable policy, his technocratic background and years under Sri Mulyani making it "a step towards strengthening stability." Achmad Nur Hidayat, a public-policy analyst at Jakarta's National Development University, noted Suahasil understands the budget, taxation, financing, subsidies and fiscal governance "from the inside." Fauzi Amro, deputy chair of parliament's Commission XI, endorsed the choice as "a solution-oriented figure," saying Suahasil would take up unfinished work on tax, customs and excise revenue and non-tax state revenue.

## The open question for Indonesia's finances

The to-do list is daunting. Suahasil must continue the unfinished agenda — optimising tax, customs and excise revenue, widening non-tax state revenue (PNBP), and straightening out central-to-regional fiscal transfers — while delivering 6 per cent growth without breaching the deficit ceiling amid global pressure and domestic challenges. Analysts note his more subdued style, and whether he can balance Prabowo's forceful agenda against markets' cautious expectations will decide the trajectory of Indonesia's fiscal credibility. A hasty reshuffle has placed the economy's two most important goals — growth and stability — on the new minister's shoulders at once.

## Further reading

**Danantara:** Indonesia's sovereign wealth fund, overseeing equity in multiple state-owned enterprises since 2025 and weighing heavily in fiscal and state-asset allocation; its boundaries with the Finance Ministry over the use of profits were a key backdrop to the reshuffle.

**APBN (State Budget):** Indonesia's annual state budget, under which the constitution caps the fiscal deficit at 3 per cent of GDP; it is the central instrument of government policy and macroeconomic stability.
