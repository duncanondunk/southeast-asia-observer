# 马来西亚"昌明经济"一周年：外资与改革的成绩单

自 2023 年安华政府推出"昌明经济"（Ekonomi Madani）框架以来，2026 年已是其落地约三年、全面执行近一周年的节点。框架以"永续、繁荣、创新、尊重"为底色，试图回答一个老问题：资源富集、地缘关键的马来西亚，如何把禀赋变成可持续的增长？一年下来，外资与改革是两份最被盯紧的成绩单。

## 框架承诺：从口号到可量化目标

昌明经济并非凭空而来。它承接了马来西亚长期未竟的结构改革议程：提升本地附加值、改善收入分配、吸引高端投资。其可量化抓手包括 GDP 增长目标、投资审批提速、公务员与补贴改革。对投资者而言，真正的看点不是修辞，而是审批效率与政策可预期性是否改善。

## 外资成绩单：电子电气与数据中心领跑

一年里，最亮眼的是投资端。马来西亚凭借成熟的电子电气（E&E）集群与相对稳定的政策，承接了来自"中国+1"与 AI 数据中心的红利，柔佛—新加坡经济特区（JS-SEZ）成为磁铁。但隐忧在于，部分投资偏重"落地即用电"的数据中心，其与本地产业链的粘连度，仍需时间检验。

## 改革成绩单：补贴与官僚的"硬骨头"

改革侧更为艰难。柴油补贴合理化、公务员编制优化、国企治理等议题触及既得利益。进展有，但速度受政治联盟的脆弱性牵制——一个依赖多党共组的政府，任何大刀阔斧都可能触发内部反弹。改革的"慢"，既是审慎，也是约束。

## 风险：地缘、汇率与"中等收入陷阱"幽灵

成绩单之外是三重风险。其一，地缘选边压力：在中美之间走平衡木，任何倾斜都可能影响特定外资流向。其二，林吉特汇率波动与输入型通胀，侵蚀家庭购买力。其三，"中等收入陷阱"的幽灵仍在——若高端投资与生产率提升跟不上，增长可能再次卡顿。

## 值得观察的几点

其一，JS-SEZ 的跨境人流与资本流动细则能否落地，决定柔佛能否成为真正增长极。其二，补贴改革会否扩围到更敏感的领域。其三，2027 年预算能否把"昌明"叙事转化为可感知的民生红利。

归根结底，昌明经济的一周年，是一份"有亮点、欠火候"的成绩单。外资用脚投票给了初步信任，改革则仍在啃硬骨头。马来西亚的考题，从来不是有没有框架，而是框架能否在政治上可持续地被执行。

## 延伸阅读

**昌明经济（Ekonomi Madani）**：马来西亚安华政府于 2023 年提出的经济框架，强调永续、繁荣、创新与尊重，意在推动结构改革与包容性增长。

**中国+1（China Plus One）**：跨国企业为分散对华单一依赖，将部分产能与投资转移至东南亚等地的策略，马来西亚是主要承接国之一。

**柔佛—新加坡经济特区（JS-SEZ）**：马来西亚柔佛州与新加坡共建的跨境经济区，以制造业、数据与服务业协同为特色，2024 年后加速推进。

# Malaysia's 'Ekonomi Madani' at One Year: The Foreign-Investment and Reform Report Card

Since the Anwar government launched the 'Ekonomi Madani' framework in 2023, 2026 marks roughly three years since its unveiling and nearly a year of full execution. Built on sustainability, prosperity, innovation and respect, it tries to answer an old question: how does resource-rich, geopolitically pivotal Malaysia turn endowments into durable growth? A year on, foreign investment and reform are the two report cards watched closest.

## The framework's promise: from slogan to measurable targets

Madani did not appear from nowhere. It inherits Malaysia's long-unfinished structural-reform agenda: raising local value-add, improving income distribution and attracting high-end investment. Its measurable levers include GDP growth targets, faster investment approvals and civil-service and subsidy reform. For investors, the real test is not rhetoric but whether approval efficiency and policy predictability improved.

## The FDI report card: electronics and data centres lead

The brightest spot over the year is investment. Riding a mature electrical-and-electronic (E&E) cluster and relatively stable policy, Malaysia captured 'China Plus One' and AI data-centre dividends, with the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ) a magnet. But the worry is that some investment skews to data centres that 'land and draw power', whose linkage to the local supply chain still needs time to prove.

## The reform report card: the hard bones of subsidy and bureaucracy

Reform is harder. Rationalising diesel subsidies, optimising civil-service size and state-firm governance touch vested interests. Progress exists but its pace is constrained by the fragility of a coalition government — any bold move in a multi-party administration can trigger internal backlash. The 'slowness' of reform is both prudence and constraint.

## Risks: geopolitics, exchange rate and the middle-income trap spectre

Beyond the cards sit three risks. First, pressure to pick sides between the US and China: any tilt may shift specific capital flows. Second, ringgit volatility and imported inflation erode household purchasing power. Third, the spectre of the 'middle-income trap' lingers — if high-end investment and productivity gains lag, growth may stall again.

## A few things to watch

First, whether JS-SEZ rules on cross-border people and capital flow land, deciding whether Johor becomes a real growth pole. Second, whether subsidy reform expands to more sensitive areas. Third, whether the 2027 budget converts the 'Madani' narrative into tangible household dividends.

In the end, Madani's first year is a report card of 'highlights, not yet maturity'. Foreign capital voted with its feet in initial trust; reform is still chewing hard bones. Malaysia's exam was never whether it has a framework, but whether that framework can be executed sustainably through politics.

## Further reading

**Ekonomi Madani**: The economic framework unveiled by Malaysia's Anwar government in 2023, stressing sustainability, prosperity, innovation and respect to drive structural reform and inclusive growth.

**China Plus One**: A strategy by multinationals to diversify away from single-country dependence on China by shifting some capacity and investment to places like Southeast Asia; Malaysia is a main recipient.

**Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ)**: A cross-border economic zone between Malaysia's Johor and Singapore, featuring manufacturing, data and services synergy, accelerated after 2024.
