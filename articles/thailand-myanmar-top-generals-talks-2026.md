# 继元首会晤后 泰缅最高军事将领会谈 边境安全议题浮上桌面

继泰国首相阿努廷本月初与缅甸领导人敏昂莱会晤后，两国最高军事将领也坐到了一起。泰国武装部队总司令乌克里斯8月20日在曼谷一家酒店会见缅甸军方领导人耶温乌，这是后者上任以来首次出访外国。

## 将帅会晤：一次"破冰"式出访

法新社报道，此次会晤选在曼谷的酒店举行，时机敏感：缅甸军方正试图摆脱国际社会长期孤立。耶温乌曾任军政府情报负责人，并因参与政变受到美国等多国制裁；联合国调查人员上周称，其领导的缅军正加大对平民目标的空袭。

泰国方面对此举自有算盘。本月初阿努廷以隆重礼遇接待敏昂莱时，外长西哈萨辩护称"这不是关乎合法性，而是面对现实"。但对缅甸问题特别顾问委员会而言，泰国"拥抱一个军阀，已经烧毁了自己的信誉"。

## 2400公里边境的溢出效应

双方预计聚焦长达2400公里的泰缅边境。缅甸内战外溢，带来难民、武装冲突，以及毒品与人口贩运等问题，持续给泰国制造安全与边境管控压力。

对曼谷而言，与内比都保持沟通渠道，既是管控边境风险的务实需要，也是平衡国内政治与区域稳定的博弈。军方层面的直接对话，意味着安全议题被摆上更高层级。

## 合法性之争与国际批评

会晤引发的人权争议不容忽视。联合国缅甸问题独立调查机制的年度报告记录了缅军长期实施任意拘押、酷刑与性暴力；缅甸政府则称报告是"针对安全部队的片面指控"，所据信息"无法核实且不准确"。

泰国允许耶温乌到访，被批评为变相承认军政府合法性。在东盟内部对缅立场分歧依旧的背景下，曼谷的这一步，既是经济与安全考量的结果，也必然付出外交信誉的代价。

## 延伸阅读

**敏昂莱（Min Aung Hlaing）**：缅甸军方首脑，2021年政变后实际执政；本月初以国家元首身份访泰，与阿努廷会晤。

**泰缅边境（Thailand–Myanmar Border）**：长约2400公里，缅甸内战的难民、毒品与人口贩运等问题长期外溢至泰国一侧。

**东盟（ASEAN）**：东南亚国家联盟；其对缅甸危机的"五点共识"执行乏力，成员国在对缅接触立场上分歧明显。

# After Leaders' Summit, Thailand and Myanmar's Top Generals Meet; Border Security Takes Center Stage

Following Thai Prime Minister Anutin's meeting with Myanmar's leader Min Aung Hlaing earlier this month, the two countries' top military commanders have now sat down together. Thailand's armed forces commander Ukris Boontanondha met Myanmar's military leader Ye Win Oo at a Bangkok hotel on August 20 — the latter's first foreign trip since taking office.

## A commander's meeting: an "ice-breaking" visit

Agence France-Presse reported the meeting was held at a Bangkok hotel at a sensitive moment: Myanmar's military is trying to shake off its long international isolation. Ye Win Oo, formerly the junta's intelligence chief, is under sanctions from the United States and others for his role in the coup; UN investigators said last week that the military he leads is intensifying airstrikes on civilian targets.

Bangkok has its own calculus. When Anutin received Min Aung Hlaing with full honors earlier this month, Foreign Minister Sihasak defended it as "not about legitimacy, but about facing reality." But to the Special Advisory Council for Myanmar, Thailand "has burned its credibility by embracing a warlord."

## The spillover along 2,400 km of border

Talks are expected to focus on the 2,400 km Thailand–Myanmar border. Myanmar's civil war spills over in refugees, armed conflict, and drug and human trafficking, all of which keep pressuring Thailand's security and border control.

For Bangkok, keeping a channel to Naypyidaw is both a practical need to manage border risk and a balancing act between domestic politics and regional stability. A direct military-to-military dialogue puts security issues on a higher rung.

## The legitimacy debate and international criticism

The human-rights controversy is hard to ignore. The UN Independent Investigative Mechanism's annual report documents the Myanmar military's long-standing arbitrary detention, torture and sexual violence; the Myanmar government calls the report "one-sided accusations" based on unverifiable and inaccurate information.

Allowing Ye Win Oo to visit is criticized as implicitly recognizing the junta's legitimacy. With ASEAN still divided on engaging Myanmar, Bangkok's step is the product of economic and security calculations — and will inevitably cost it diplomatic credibility.

## Further reading

**Min Aung Hlaing**: Myanmar's military chief who has effectively ruled since the 2021 coup; he visited Thailand as head of state earlier this month and met Anutin.

**Thailand–Myanmar Border**: about 2,400 km long, where refugees, drugs and human trafficking from Myanmar's civil war have long spilled into Thailand.

**ASEAN**: the Association of Southeast Asian Nations; its "Five-Point Consensus" on Myanmar has stalled, and members remain split on engaging the junta.
