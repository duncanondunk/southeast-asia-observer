# 侨乡"侨"字号产业回乡：从捐赠到投资

在中国东南沿海的侨乡，一种新现象正在沉淀：华侨华人的回归，正从"捐钱修桥"的单向慈善，转向"带资回乡"的双向产业。2026 年，在高质量发展与"万侨创新"的语境下，"侨"字号产业——由侨资、侨智、侨技驱动的经济形态——正成为侨乡转型升级的一支新变量。

## 从捐赠到投资：动机的迁移

早期侨乡与侨胞的关系是"捐建逻辑"：学校、医院、道路，由侨胞解囊。如今逻辑在变。新一代侨商带着资本、技术与全球网络回流，投资对象从公益设施转向产业园区、跨境电商、现代农业。动机也从"造福桑梓"的情怀，叠加了"寻找新增长曲线"的理性——侨乡的低要素成本与政策礼包，正与侨商的全球渠道形成互补。

## "侨"字号产业长什么样

所谓"侨"字号产业，并非简单挂名。它往往具备三个特征：资本来自侨资、技术或管理来自侨智、市场连接侨胞的全球网络。典型形态包括侨乡跨境电商综试区、侨商产业园、华侨华人创新创业基地。在汕头、江门、泉州等地，这类平台正把"侨"从身份符号，转化为可计量的经济资产。

## 侨乡如何"接得住"

侨资回乡，考验的是侨乡的承接力。其一是制度供给：侨商投资的审批、用地、汇兑便利度。其二是服务供给：从语言、法律到子女教育的"软配套"。其三是信任供给：侨胞最在意的，是家乡政策的可预期与兑现度。哪里的"侨务营商环境"更稳，侨资就往哪里聚。

## 隐忧：情怀不能替代回报

热络之下有冷静一面。侨资回乡并非只讲情怀——它同样是资本，要求回报与退出机制。若侨乡只把侨胞当"提款机"，而缺乏可持续的商业生态，回归可能昙花一现。更有甚者，个别"侨牌"项目沦为圈地圈钱的噱头，反伤侨胞信任。

## 值得观察的几点

其一，侨商产业园的真实入驻率与产出，而非挂牌数量。其二，侨资与本地产业链的咬合度，能否带动就业与技术外溢。其三，"侨"字号能否从沿海侨乡，向内陆侨源城市扩散。

归根结底，侨乡"侨"字号产业的回乡，是一场从情感纽带到价值纽带的升级。它让侨胞的回归不止于"回来看看"，而能"回来干实事"。当捐赠的逻辑让位于投资的逻辑，侨乡与侨胞的关系，正被重新书写。

## 延伸阅读

**侨乡（Qiaoxiang）**：历史上大量居民移居海外的中国县市，如广东台山、福建泉州、浙江青田，与海外侨胞保持密切经济文化联系。

**万侨创新**：中国侨务系统推动华侨华人参与国家创新发展的工作框架，强调侨资、侨智与双创结合。

**侨资（Overseas Chinese Capital）**：华侨华人拥有的可投资资本，常通过回乡投资、基金、产业园等形式进入侨乡与国内。

# Qiaoxiang's 'Qiao-Brand' Industries Return Home: From Donations to Investment

In the qiaoxiang (ancestral hometowns) along China's southeast coast, a new phenomenon is settling: the return of overseas Chinese is shifting from one-way charity of 'donating for bridges' to two-way industry of 'capital coming home'. In 2026, under high-quality development and a 'ten-thousand qiao innovating' framing, 'qiao-brand' industries — economic forms driven by qiao capital, qiao talent and qiao technology — are becoming a new variable in qiaoxiang transformation.

## From donations to investment: a shift in motive

The early qiaoxiang–diaspora tie was 'donation logic': schools, hospitals, roads funded by overseas kin. The logic is changing. A new generation of qiao entrepreneurs returns with capital, technology and global networks, steering investment from public facilities to industrial parks, cross-border e-commerce and modern agriculture. The motive migrates from 'benevolence to homeland' sentiment to a rational 'search for new growth curves' — qiaoxiang's lower factor costs and policy gifts complement qiao merchants' global channels.

## What 'qiao-brand' industry looks like

A 'qiao-brand' industry is not a mere name tag. It usually has three traits: capital from qiao capital, technology or management from qiao talent, and market links to diaspora global networks. Typical forms include qiaoxiang cross-border e-commerce zones, qiao-merchant industrial parks and overseas-Chinese innovation bases. In Shantou, Jiangmen and Quanzhou, such platforms turn 'qiao' from an identity symbol into a measurable economic asset.

## How qiaoxiang 'catches' them

Qiao capital's return tests qiaoxiang's absorptive capacity. First, institutional supply: approval, land and exchange convenience for qiao investment. Second, service supply: soft amenities from language and law to children's education. Third, trust supply: what qiao care about most is the predictability and fulfilment of hometown policy. Wherever the 'qiao-business environment' is steadier, qiao capital gathers.

## The worry: sentiment cannot replace returns

Under the warmth lies sobriety. Qiao capital's return is not only about sentiment — it is still capital demanding returns and exit mechanisms. If qiaoxiang treats diaspora only as an ATM while lacking a sustainable business ecosystem, the return may be fleeting. Worse, some 'qiao-brand' projects become land-or-money grabs in disguise, harming diaspora trust.

## A few things to watch

First, the real occupancy and output of qiao-merchant parks, not their plaque count. Second, how tightly qiao capital meshes with local supply chains, whether it drives jobs and technology spillover. Third, whether 'qiao-brand' spreads from coastal qiaoxiang to inland qiao-source cities.

In the end, qiaoxiang's 'qiao-brand' industries returning home is an upgrade from emotional to value ties. It lets diaspora return not only to 'come visit' but to 'come do real work'. As donation logic yields to investment logic, the qiaoxiang–diaspora relationship is being rewritten.

## Further reading

**Qiaoxiang**: Chinese counties and cities with historically large emigrant populations, such as Taishan in Guangdong, Quanzhou in Fujian and Qingtian in Zhejiang, maintaining close economic and cultural ties with overseas kin.

**Ten-Thousand Qiao Innovating**: A Chinese qiao-affairs framework promoting overseas Chinese participation in national innovation, emphasising the blend of qiao capital, qiao talent and mass entrepreneurship.

**Overseas Chinese Capital (Qiao Capital)**: Investable capital owned by overseas Chinese, often entering qiaoxiang and domestic markets via return investment, funds and industrial parks.
